---
title: Use a MIDI controller
description: Drive a flow from a MIDI keyboard or knob box, or play notes out to a synth.
---

The [MIDI node](/docs/microflow-studio/nodes/sense/midi) connects your flow to MIDI devices attached to your **computer**. A knob on a controller becomes a value in your flow; a sensor on your desk becomes a note on a synth.

## When you need it

- You have a controller with more knobs and buttons than you want to wire on a breadboard.
- You are building an instrument and want your sensors to play something.
- You want a physical interface for a prototype in five minutes rather than an afternoon.

## Before you start

Plug the controller into your computer, not into the Arduino, and confirm your operating system sees it:

- **macOS** — Audio MIDI Setup → Window → Show MIDI Studio.
- **Windows** — Device Manager → Sound, video and game controllers.

If your computer cannot see it, Studio cannot either.

## Receive from a controller

1. Press `Cmd/Ctrl + K` and add a **MIDI** node.
2. Set **Direction** to `in`.
3. Pick your controller in **Device**.
4. Leave **Channel** at `0` to start — that means every channel.
5. Set **Mode**:
   - `note` for keys and pads. Use the `note`, `velocity`, `on`, and `off` outputs.
   - `cc` for knobs and faders. Set **Control** to the controller number and read the `value` output.
6. Hang a [Monitor](/docs/microflow-studio/nodes/express/monitor) on the outputs and move something on the controller.

**Finding a knob's control number:** set **Mode** to `cc`, put a Monitor on `value`, and try control numbers while turning the knob. Most controllers also print their mapping in the manual, which is quicker.

## Send to a synth

1. Set **Direction** to `out` and pick the destination device.
2. Set **Mode** to `note` and choose the **Note** and **Velocity**, or `song` to play a sequence at a **Tempo**.
3. Wire something into the `send` input — a [Button](/docs/microflow-studio/nodes/sense/button), an [Interval](/docs/microflow-studio/nodes/generate/interval), or a [Compare](/docs/microflow-studio/nodes/decide/compare) firing on a threshold.

To play a note from a sensor reading, put a [Map](/docs/microflow-studio/nodes/shape/map) node in between: MIDI notes run `0`–`127`, and so do velocity and CC values.

## Verify it worked

Receiving: turn a knob and watch the Monitor. Nothing arriving? Set **Channel** to `0`, then check the mode matches what your device sends — a knob sending CC will produce nothing at all in note mode.

Sending: put the synth somewhere you can hear it and fire the `send` input by hand from a [Constant](/docs/microflow-studio/nodes/generate/constant) or a [Hotkey](/docs/microflow-studio/nodes/sense/hotkey) node.

## Gotchas

- **The device list is a snapshot.** Plug a controller in after opening the settings and it will not appear until you reopen them.
- **Serial MIDI on the board is a different thing.** If you generate a sketch that uses MIDI on the board itself, it claims the board's hardware serial and needs a physical DIN-5 jack or shield — see [the sketch warning](/docs/troubleshooting/sketch-warnings#midi). A USB controller plugged into your computer is unaffected.
- **Channel `0` means all channels.** Narrow it once you know which one your device uses.
