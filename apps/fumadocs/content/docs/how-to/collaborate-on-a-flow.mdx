Several people can open the same flow and see each other's changes as they happen. Access is granted per person, by email.

## What you need

- A Microflow account and a saved flow. The **Local Flow** cannot be shared. It lives in your browser.
- An email address for each person you invite.

## Steps

1. Open the flow, then open its settings from the dock at the bottom of the canvas.
2. Find the **Collaborators** section and click **Share**.
3. Enter the person's email address.
4. Select a role:
   - **Editor** — can change the flow.
   - **Viewer** — can open, pan, zoom, and select, and can change nothing.
5. Submit. A person with an account is added at once. A person without one shows as **Invited** and gets access when they sign up.
6. Send them the link from the same dialog.

<Callout type="warn" title="The link grants no access">
Sending the link to someone you have not added does nothing for them. The link opens the flow for people who already have access. The email invite grants the access.
</Callout>

## Verify it worked

The person appears in the collaborators table with their role. When they open the flow, their cursor appears on your canvas with their name beside it.

## Change or remove access

Change a role from the row's menu on the same page. The change applies at once: a viewer gets a **View only** badge, their nodes stop moving, and the delete key stops working.

You can withdraw a pending invite before it is accepted. Only the flow's owner can invite people, change roles, or delete the flow.

## Who runs the hardware

The flow is shared. The hardware is not.

The board is plugged into one person's machine, so every other sidebar correctly reports no microcontroller.

Live values are local too. Each person's Monitor nodes show their own machine's runtime, so a collaborator without a board sees the flow but no readings. See [Collaborate without hardware](/docs/how-to/collaborate-without-hardware).

## What stays in sync

**Synced:** node positions, node settings, edges, each person's cursor, and each person's selection.

**Per machine:** which board is connected, the live values on the nodes, and your zoom and pan.

## Conflicts

Microflow uses [Yjs](https://yjs.dev), so simultaneous edits merge instead of failing. If two people change one setting at the same instant, one change wins. There is no lock and no merge dialog, and you cannot lose the document. Nothing stops two people undoing each other's work, so agree who edits what.

Edits made offline are stored locally and merge when you reconnect. There is no save button.
