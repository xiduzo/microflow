---
title: MQTT
description: Publish values from your flow to an MQTT topic, or feed incoming MQTT messages into your flow.
---

The MQTT node connects your flow to an MQTT broker — a message-passing server. Its **direction** setting decides what it does:

- **subscribe** — the node listens to a topic and sends each message it receives out of its output.
- **publish** — the node sends each value that arrives at its input to a topic.

<Callout title="Linking to Figma or Penpot? Use the Design variable node">
To read or change a variable in a Figma or Penpot file, use the [Design variable](/docs/microflow-studio/nodes/express/figma) node. It works with the Figma and Penpot plugins, and you do not need to know their topics.
</Callout>

Before you use the node, add a broker under **Configuration → MQTT** in Studio. A broker has a name, a URL, and an optional username and password. In the browser, Studio can only reach a broker over `ws://` or `wss://`. The desktop app also accepts other schemes, such as `mqtt://`.

<Callout type="warn" title="Public brokers mean public data">
Anyone can read and send messages on a public broker such as `test.mosquitto.org`. Do not send passwords, personal information, or anything sensitive through it. It is fine for testing and prototyping.
</Callout>

## Settings

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Broker** | Which of your brokers to use |
| **direction** | `subscribe` to receive messages, `publish` to send them |
| **topic** | The topic to subscribe or publish to |
| **QoS** | The delivery guarantee: at most once (0), at least once (1), or exactly once (2) |
| **retain** | Publish only. Asks the broker to keep the last message for new subscribers |
