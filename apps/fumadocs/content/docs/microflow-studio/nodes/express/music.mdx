---
title: Music
description: Play songs from your computer through its speakers.
---

The Music node plays songs from your own machine. Where the [Piezo](/docs/microflow-studio/nodes/express/piezo) makes simple electronic tones on your board, Music plays real audio — mp3, wav, or anything else your browser can play — through the speakers of the computer running Microflow.

One node holds a stack of records, so it can act as a jukebox for a whole project rather than a single sound effect.

**Example uses:**
- Play a jingle when a sensor is triggered
- Give each button on your prototype its own song
- Step through a playlist with a counter or a dial

## What you can do

- **Play** — start the selected record from the beginning. Triggering it again while it plays restarts it.
- **Stop** — stop playback.
- **Set** — select a record without playing it, by name or by number (0 is the first record). Wire a counter or a dial here to step through your songs.
- **One handle per song** — every record you add gets its own handle on the node, so you can wire a trigger straight to the song it should play. It selects that record and plays it in one go.
- **Value** — true while a song is playing, false once it stops or reaches the end.
- **Track** — the name of the selected record, so you can show it on a monitor.

## Settings

Open the settings panel to configure:

- **Add songs** — pick one or more audio files from your computer. Files are stored inside the flow itself, so they travel with it when you share it — which is why each file must stay under 2 MB, and all of them together under 6 MB.
- **Record** — which song is selected
- **Play / Stop** — try the selected record without wiring anything up
- **Remove song** — remove the selected record
- **Volume** — playback volume, from 0 to 1
- **Loop** — restart the song when it ends instead of stopping

## Good to know

Songs play on the computer running Microflow, not on your board — so the node works with or without hardware connected, and it is left out when you export your flow as an Arduino sketch.

Only the first eight records get their own handle, to keep the node readable. Beyond that, select records through **Set**.
