---
title: Internal
description: Internal nodes help you understand and document your flow — monitoring and notes.
---

Internal nodes do not affect how your flow runs and do not appear in the **Add node** palette — you add a Note from the canvas itself.

Looking for the value graph? That is the [Monitor](/docs/microflow-studio/nodes/express/monitor) node, under Express.

| Node | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [Note](/docs/microflow-studio/nodes/internal/note) | A text note you can place anywhere on the canvas |
