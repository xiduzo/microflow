Microflow Studio is where you build. Drag [nodes](/docs/microflow-studio/nodes) onto a canvas, connect them with [edges](/docs/microflow-studio/edges), and Studio runs the whole thing on your board over USB. No compile step, no run button — edit while it runs and the change applies immediately.

It comes in two forms, and they do the same thing:

- **The desktop app** — Windows, macOS, and Linux. Auto-detects your board. Required for MQTT and AI provider configuration. [Download it](https://github.com/xiduzo/microflow-t-stack/releases).
- **The web app** — Chrome, Edge, or Opera, with nothing to install. See [Use Studio in your browser](/docs/how-to/use-studio-in-your-browser).

## The window

Three regions.

### The sidebar

Everything *about* your flow, top to bottom:

- **Flow switcher** — every flow you have, and the button to make a new one. `Cmd/Ctrl + 1`–`9` jumps between them.
- **Microcontroller status** — which board is connected, or why none is. The question mark beside it opens [Board not detected](/docs/troubleshooting/board-not-detected).
- **The current flow** — its three views: **Edit flow**, **Show circuit**, **Show code**.
- **General** — **My flows**, **Templates**, and **Configuration** for [MQTT brokers](/docs/how-to/run-your-own-mqtt-broker) and [AI providers](/docs/how-to/connect-a-local-llm). Those are set once and shared by every flow.

### The canvas

The flow itself.

- **Add a node** — `Cmd/Ctrl + K`, or the `+` in the dock. The palette is searchable: type what the thing does, not what it is called.
- **Connect nodes** — drag from a handle on the right of one node to a handle on the left of another.
- **Move** — drag a node. **Pan** — drag empty canvas. **Zoom** — scroll.
- **Find your way back** — `Shift + 1` fits everything, or just what you have selected.

Each node shows its live value while the flow runs. That is the fastest debugging tool in the app — see [Debug with the Monitor node](/docs/how-to/debug-with-monitor-node).

### The dock

Along the bottom: **add node**, **undo**, **redo**, **export this flow to a file**, and **flow settings** — where you rename a flow, [invite collaborators](/docs/how-to/collaborate-on-a-flow), and delete it. Undo and redo dim when there is nothing left to do.

## Three views of one flow

| View | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Edit flow** | Building. The canvas. |
| **Show circuit** | The wiring your flow implies, pin by pin. Build the physical side from it — see [Read the circuit view](/docs/how-to/read-the-circuit-view). |
| **Show code** | The Arduino sketch your flow compiles to, downloadable as a `.ino` file — see [Export an Arduino sketch](/docs/how-to/export-an-arduino-sketch). |

The circuit and the code are generated from the flow every time you open them, so they cannot fall out of date.
