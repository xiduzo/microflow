Find the line that matches what you are seeing. Each one goes straight to the checks for that symptom, ordered by how often each cause is the real one.

## Connecting to a board

| What you see | Go to |
| --- | --- |
| Sidebar says **No microcontroller connected** with the board plugged in | [Board not detected](/docs/troubleshooting/board-not-detected) |
| Sidebar says **Connect board** and clicking it does nothing lasting | [Board not detected — in a browser](/docs/troubleshooting/board-not-detected#5-in-a-browser) |
| Sidebar says **Use Chrome/Edge or the desktop app** | You are in Firefox or Safari. [Use Studio in your browser](/docs/how-to/use-studio-in-your-browser) |
| Stuck on **Flashing firmware** | Wait — up to a minute is normal. Longer: [Board not detected](/docs/troubleshooting/board-not-detected) |
| Sidebar says **Error** | [Board not detected](/docs/troubleshooting/board-not-detected) |
| Connected, then dropped out on its own | [Flow stops mid-run](/docs/troubleshooting/flow-stops-mid-run) |

## None of these

Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/xiduzo/microflow-t-stack/issues) with:

- Your operating system, and whether you are in the desktop app or a browser
- The Microflow Studio version
- What you expected, and what happened instead
- Whether you can reproduce it from an empty canvas, and the steps

The [contributing guide](/docs/contributing/report-bug-or-request) has more on what makes a report actionable.
