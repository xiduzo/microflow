## Collaboration

| What you see | Go to |
| --- | --- |
| A **View only** badge, and nothing moves | You have the viewer role. See [Collaborate on a flow](/docs/how-to/collaborate-on-a-flow) |
| Your collaborator's sidebar reports no board, and their Monitors are empty | Correct. Hardware and live values are local. See [Collaborate without hardware](/docs/how-to/collaborate-without-hardware) |

## None of these

Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/xiduzo/microflow-t-stack/issues) with:

- Your operating system, and whether you use the desktop app or a browser.
- The Microflow Studio version.
- What you expected, and what happened.
- The steps to reproduce it from an empty canvas.

See the [contributing guide](/docs/contributing/report-bug-or-request) for what makes a report actionable.
