So far every value has been on or off. A potentiometer reports a range: turning the knob slides the value between two extremes. In this lesson the knob sets the LED's brightness.

## What you need

- A 10 kΩ rotary potentiometer, with three legs.
- An LED and a 220 Ω resistor.
- A breadboard and jumper wires.
- A board [connected to Studio](/docs/how-to/connect-a-microcontroller).

<Callout type="info" title="Template available">
Studio ships this flow as the **Potentiometer Fade** template, under **Templates**.
</Callout>

## Step 1 — Wire the potentiometer

A potentiometer has two outer legs and a middle leg, called the wiper. The outer legs take power and ground. The wiper reports a voltage between them.

| Connection | From | To |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Power | 5V | One outer leg |
| Ground | GND | The other outer leg |
| Signal | The middle leg | **A0** |

Swapping the two outer legs reverses the direction of the reading. It damages nothing.

## Step 2 — Wire the LED

The LED needs a PWM pin, because dimming needs a partial output. On an Uno, the PWM pins are 3, 5, 6, 9, 10, and 11, marked `~` on the board.

| Connection | From | To |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signal | Pin 9 | The 220 Ω resistor, then the long leg of the LED |
| Ground | The short leg of the LED | GND |

## Step 3 — Build the flow

1. Add a [Potentiometer](/docs/microflow-studio/nodes/sense/potentiometer) node and set **Pin** to `A0`.
2. Add a [Map](/docs/microflow-studio/nodes/shape/map) node.
3. Add an [LED](/docs/microflow-studio/nodes/express/led) node and set **Pin** to `9`.
4. Connect the Potentiometer node's `value` output to the Map node's `value` input.
5. Connect the Map node's `to` output to the LED node's `value` input.

Now set the ranges in the Map node:

- **From** — min `0`, max `1023`. That is what an analog pin reads.
- **To** — min `0`, max `255`. That is what LED brightness accepts.

## Step 4 — Test it

Turn the knob. The LED fades between off and full brightness across the whole travel of the knob.

If it does not, check these in order.

| Symptom | Cause |
| --- | --- |
| The LED reaches full brightness in the first quarter turn | The Map node is missing, or its ranges are wrong. |
| The LED will not dim, only switch | The LED is on a pin without PWM. Move it to 3, 5, 6, 9, 10, or 11. |
| The LED stays dark or stays bright | 5 V and ground are swapped on the outer legs, or the wiper is on an outer leg. |

## What just happened

Your flow now uses three categories of node:

- **Sense** — the Potentiometer node reads the world.
- **Shape** — the Map node rescales the value.
- **Express** — the LED node acts on the world.

The Map node does one small job: it converts a number from one range into another. Reach for it whenever a sensor's range does not match what the next node expects.
