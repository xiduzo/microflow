Each lesson is short, hands-on, and ends with something that works. Work through them in order. Every lesson builds on the one before it.

You need a computer, an **Arduino Uno**, an **Arduino Nano**, or an **ESP32**, and a USB cable that carries data. Each lesson lists any extra parts at the top.

## How to use these lessons

- **Do the steps yourself.** Reading is not doing.
- **Check each result before you continue.** When a step says the LED switches on, confirm it. If it does not, the next step will not work either.
- **Do not expect to understand everything yet.** The [Concepts](/docs/concepts) section explains why things work.

All six lessons together use every category of node.
